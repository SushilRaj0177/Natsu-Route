## Door to door at 13:00

114 random trips 1.5–7 km apart inside the wards, planned with the timetables of 2026-10-12 (a public holiday) and that day's heat and sun. NatsuRoute offered a cooler option than the fastest in 85 of them.

- Median time in the sun: fastest 11.1 min, coolest 5.8 min
- Median minutes of sun saved: 4; mean cut where the fastest had at least a minute of sun: 36.9%
- Median extra door-to-door time when a cooler option was offered: 5.8 min

## Limits

Shade comes from PLATEAU buildings and decks and from the trees PLATEAU and OpenStreetMap have; awnings, signs and clouds are not modelled, and tree cover is incomplete, so real streets are somewhat shadier than this. Results describe the model, not measured exposure.
